§ 01 ─ ABOUT

I am a second-year Bachelor of Business Administration student at Macquarie University and the digital operations and AI automation lead for CHAN NEUNG, my family's premium Thai fruit brand based in Bangkok.

I bridge hands-on retail experience with practical AI and automation. Day to day, that means designing AI ordering agents that customers actually use, connecting six online sales channels into one operating picture, and applying upstream thinking to fix supply chain problems before they reach the shelf.

I value clear thinking, accountable decisions, and the discipline of fixing causes rather than symptoms.

§ 01.A ─ PROCEDURES

What I run, day to day

Three operating procedures from CHAN NEUNG. Each is documented in the same shape: problem, approach, outcome, runtime metadata.

  1. PROC 01 ─ AI Ordering Agent
    SHIPPED

    PROBLEM

    Customers ordering through Facebook Messenger required manual responses for every order, including address capture and payment confirmation. Manual order processing didn't scale.

    APPROACH

    Designed and shipped a 24/7 conversational ordering agent on Facebook Messenger using n8n cloud + OpenAI gpt-4o-mini. The agent handles address capture, payment confirmation, generates a PDF shipping label, and logs every order to a Google Sheet end-to-end.

    OUTCOME

    Order intake runs without human touch around the clock. Every order has structured data in one Google Sheet from the moment the customer confirms.

    META

    • Surface Facebook Messenger
    • Stack n8n + OpenAI gpt-4o-mini
    • Operates 24/7
  2. PROC 02 ─ Multi-Channel Pivot
    SHIPPED

    PROBLEM

    The family business operated across 20-plus Bangkok mall branches (Siam Paragon, EmSphere, ICONSIAM-class). Online presence was minimal.

    APPROACH

    Led the pivot toward an online-led strategy across six channels: Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, LINE Official Account, Facebook, and Instagram. Each channel feeds into the same Google Sheet schema as the AI ordering agent.

    OUTCOME

    Online channels now run alongside the physical mall presence with a unified back-office view of orders, regardless of source.

    META

    • Channels Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, LINE OA, FB, IG
    • Source of truth Single Google Sheet
    • Status Live
  3. PROC 03 ─ Branch & Sourcing Reform
    ITERATING

    PROBLEM

    Sell-through at our Bangkok urban branch was declining. Stock was sitting longer than usual and customer complaints were rising.

    APPROACH

    Diagnosed the issue upstream: one orchard supplier was shipping product that did not meet our spec, visibly different from product sourced through other partners. Cancelled outstanding orders from that supplier, redistributed stock from sister branches to keep the urban branch serving customers, consolidated future sourcing through suppliers who consistently met spec, and introduced a short incoming-quality checklist that branch staff complete before accepting deliveries.

    OUTCOME

    Sell-through and customer feedback at the Bangkok urban branch have improved meaningfully. The quality checklist is now standard practice. Continuing to monitor and adjust the process.

    META

    • Locus Bangkok urban branch
    • Method Stock redistribution + supplier consolidation + quality checklist
    • Status Iterating
